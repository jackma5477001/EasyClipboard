# EasyClipboard 应用图标

使用 Codex 内置 AI 绘图工具生成，随后通过 macOS 自带的 `sips` 导出 AppIcon 所需像素尺寸，保持图案与透明背景。

- 主图：[easyclipboard-icon.png](easyclipboard-icon.png)
- 样式：蓝色剪贴板、两张叠放的白色内容卡片、浅银色圆角底板。
- AppIcon：16、32、128、256、512 点的 1x/2x 版本，最大 1024×1024 像素。
- 菜单栏与面板内的小图标继续使用原生 SF Symbols；此图片用于应用图标。

## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: production macOS application icon for EasyClipboard, a local clipboard-history utility.
Primary request: a single beautifully crafted app icon, square 1024 x 1024 pixels, suitable for the macOS Dock, Finder and application switcher.
Subject: an instantly recognizable cobalt-blue clipboard with a rounded top clip, holding two slightly offset pearl-white content cards. The visible front card has three simple bold horizontal blue marks to suggest saved copied content. The overlapping cards communicate clipboard history. No letters or numbers.
Style: restrained contemporary native macOS icon craftsmanship, front-facing orthographic composition, soft dimensional depth, precise rounded geometry and subtle ceramic / satin glass finish. Harmonize with a pale silver and system-blue native utility UI.
Composition: ONE icon only, centered, a rounded-square pearl-white / very pale silver tile occupying approximately 84% of the 1024 canvas, leaving balanced equal transparent margin on all four sides. A large simple clipboard glyph occupies about 65% of the tile. Strong recognizable silhouette and contrast even at 16 x 16 pixels. Slightly overlapping cards must remain simple and readable at small sizes.
Color palette: saturated system blue / cobalt for the clipboard body, pale blue edge highlights, clean white cards, a softly lit pale silver tile. Restrained soft shadow and gentle beveled edges, no intense glow.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha outside the rounded-square tile and its subtle tight shadow; transparent corners. Do not place the icon on a white presentation sheet or a checkerboard.
Constraints: no text, no app name, no typography, no watermark, no device, no UI screenshot, no arrows, no badges, no checkmarks, no cloud, no extra icons, no multi-option grid, no decorative tiny details, no perspective tilt. The output is the final icon artwork itself, with large clear shapes and crisp edges.

## 调整提示词

Use case: precise-object-edit.
Edit target: the attached EasyClipboard macOS app icon.
Make only this cleanup: remove every stray isolated white or colored pixel, fleck, fragment, halo and rough fringe around the outside of the rounded-square tile. Give the outer tile a perfectly clean smooth continuous rounded-square silhouette and fully transparent alpha outside it, including all corners and the balanced transparent margins. No drop shadow outside the silhouette; depth and subtle shading can remain inside the tile.
Preserve the icon design exactly: the pearl-white rounded-square tile, saturated blue clipboard body with its rounded top clip and circular inset, two overlapping white content cards, the three bold blue strokes, the same front-facing composition, proportions and materials. Do not redesign, add symbols, change the palette, add typography, add a backdrop or add a checkerboard. One square icon artwork only. Clean professionally finished icon edges for small macOS Dock sizes.
